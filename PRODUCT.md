# Human V2

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

People exploring biological systems through repeatable optimization experiments.
No clinical or professional medical use is supported.

## Product Purpose

Human V2 helps learners compare simultaneous body changes through coupled,
reproducible acute physiology experiments, a visible body and recorded causal
observations, with unsupported yearly and fatal outcomes kept explicit.

## Positioning

Model-optimization contests applied to an educational body model. Success means
understanding why an upgrade helps one benchmark and hurts another, rather than
maximizing an unexplained strength statistic.

## Operating Context

Fork baseline → modify → benchmark → compare → diagnose → iterate → submit.
First challenge maximizes sustainable workload with three modifications or fewer,
mass and resting energy within 5% of baseline, and model limits satisfied.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Five systems: heart, lungs, circulation, muscles, cooling. Bounded properties,
explicit animal-inspired presets, deterministic rest/exertion tests, baseline
score 100, comparison, history, and reproducible challenge rankings.
Simulation assumptions and unsupported interactions must be visible. No arbitrary
DNA, complete cellular simulation, transplant compatibility, clinical predictions,
or credible lifespan estimates. LLMs are unnecessary for the MVP and never own
effects, calculations, scores, or validity.

## Product Principles

- Model dependencies and resource costs together.
- Explain constraints with recorded operands and counterfactuals.
- Invalid high scores never enter rankings.
- Preserve versions and conditions so experiments can be reproduced.
- Expose unsupported interactions instead of inventing biological detail.

## Consequences Over Time

Human V2 should follow a modified person through an experience and its aftermath.
The central question is what the modification enables, what it costs during the
experience, what else must change, and what persists afterward.

Experiments carry person-state through changing exposure, activity and recovery.
Baseline and modified configurations face the same versioned scenario. Results
explain when effects emerge, what accumulates, and which modeled dependency
caused the divergence. Capacity and actual utilization remain distinct.

Owner clarified the requested experience as “living for a long time.” The
scenario follows repeated daily conditions and modeled accumulated effects,
with explicit limits on what its equations support. The original implementation
is a versioned repeated-day heat/fluid experiment:
8 h rest, 4 h activity, 12 h recovery, repeated for 1–90 days. Temperate, hot-shift
and scarce-water conditions share the same body and energy balance.

Stored heat and unreplaced sweat fluid carry across phases and days. Actual
cooling use consumes water; insufficient replacement lowers an assumed
evaporative ceiling. Rest and replacement can recover state through the same
equations. Identical conditions, matched-duration comparisons, phase ledgers,
conditional remove-one-edit replays and checked JSON replay make causes
inspectable. Saved experiments include optional versioned daily conditions.
Original challenge benchmarks, histories and rankings retain their versions.

Simulated elapsed time must be distinguished from validated predictive horizon.
No predictive horizon has been biologically validated for this illustration.
Repeating 90 days does not create an aging model. Unsupported chronic damage,
adaptation and lifespan remain unknown. At the illustrative heat/fluid model
boundary, simulation stops and leaves the remaining time unknown.

Temporal equations, units, physical provenance and chosen response curves are
listed beside the results. Mechanistic sources do not calibrate the game's
coefficients. No universal damage penalty, clinical survival decision or
invented long-term consequence is permitted. Unsupported effects stay unknown.

## Life Theatre

The active workbench lets learners combine all six supported traits and daily
conditions, simulate, and inspect an animated person in a phase-aware world.
Original SVG person art, optional system overlay, work/heat/fluid telemetry,
play/pause/restart/speed/scrubbing and matched baseline charts share recorded
time. Motion illustrates modeled activity, not measured organ rates. Display
interpolation never calculates scores or extrapolates beyond a boundary.

Editable-day-2.0.0 routines have 2–8 phases totaling 24 hours. Workload, ambient
temperature/humidity and offered sweat-fluid replacement vary by phase; stored
state carries through every phase/day. Joint sandbox changes may exceed the
challenge's three-edit budget; invalid scores remain excluded from rankings.
Routine undo, save/fork/restore, full versioned export/import and independently
recomputed replay preserve reproducibility. Legacy preset scenarios retain their
original inputs, outputs and versions. No new physiological mechanism is claimed.

## Current native physiology home — 2026-10-02

The active Home now connects to locally qualified Pulse4.4.0 through the local
Node server. Six bounded mechanism controls combine exchange area, total lung
capacity, respiratory drive, cardiac contraction, heart-rate drive and systemic
vessel resistance. Identical activity and atmospheric pressure drive matched
baseline/modified trajectories. Body pulses follow recorded heart/breathing rates;
other movement illustrates activity. Dense30-second means avoid cardiac-phase
artifacts. Audited oxygen/respiratory-alkalosis flags retain native entry timestamps
and separately labeled subsequent sample readings. Export retains raw data,
configuration, units, engine source and adapter/reference hash.

This is local acute engine integration, not independent clinical calibration
of arbitrary organ redesign. Tissue mass, chest space, surgery, adaptation and
chronic damage remain unknown. No universal penalty, headline health score,
sustainable-workload calculation, individual death year or failing-organ claim.
NCHS annual model/source and prior workbench remain preserved in source; neither
is silently applied to modified-body longevity. Native execution does not run
in the Cloudflare Worker; no production deployment is configured.
